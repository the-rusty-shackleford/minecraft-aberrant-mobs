# D-0006 -- The bite passes through the damage pipeline, finite, so miracles and totems work

**Decision**: the bite is `victim.hurt(new DamageSource(aberrantmobs:devoured, creature),
1.0e6f)`: a finite amount, beyond any absorption, through the ordinary pipeline. The
damage type is tagged `bypasses_armor`, `bypasses_effects`, `bypasses_enchantments`,
`bypasses_shield`, `bypasses_cooldown` and `no_knockback`, and deliberately not
`bypasses_invulnerability`. Never `setHealth(0)`, never `kill()`. The grab makes the
target a passenger held at the maw (`positionRider`), never a controlling one, and the
`Grip` cancels `EntityMountEvent` dismounts while held. Alive after the bite, the victim
is known to have survived (`grab.survived`) and the tree releases them, sets a
ten-minute `no_bite` and flees.

**Why**: Rusty: "if it catches and grabs you with its pincers it eats you in one bite
regardless of armour -- but Miracle Bringer must still work". Miracle Bringer answers
`LivingDamageEvent.Pre` for finite damage at or above the victim's health plus
absorption and refuses infinities; a totem is checked after the health drops and is
skipped only for `bypasses_invulnerability`. A finite million through `hurt` reaches
both. A passenger's move packets carry rotation only and the creature never returns a
controlling passenger, so there is no position fight and no "moved wrongly"; cancelling
the dismount event is the one hook the grip needs.

**Alternatives rejected**: *`setHealth(0)` or `kill()`* -- no event, no miracle, no
totem; *`Float.MAX_VALUE`* -- Miracle Bringer refuses non-finite and near-infinite
amounts; *`bypasses_invulnerability`* -- skips totems and kills creative players;
*holding by teleporting the player each tick* -- fights the client's own movement.
