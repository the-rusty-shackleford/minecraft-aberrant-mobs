# D-0007 -- The face is the last victim's profile, worn on the mask

**Decision**: when the bite kills a player, the creature keeps their `GameProfile` as
two synced strings (name, id) saved in NBT, until its next victim. The profile names
its mask cube (`rig.mask`, `head_mask`); the client's `Skin` keeps that cube's front
face corners, and `AberrantRenderer` draws two quads over it after the body: the
victim's skin's face (8, 8)-(16, 16) as cutout and the hat layer (40, 8)-(48, 16) as
translucent a hair in front, the skin from `SkinManager.getInsecureSkin` (the default
skin until the real one loads). Before any victim, nfx's painted mask shows.

**Why**: Rusty: "The Face-Stealer in the world should wear the face of its last
victim." A name and an id are all a skin lookup needs and all a save needs; the client
already knows how to fetch and cache skins. Two quads over the mask cost nothing per
frame and touch nothing of the friend's file (D-0002). The Stolen Face drop carries the
same profile in phase 6.

**Alternatives rejected**: *a `ResolvableProfile` entity-data serializer* -- none
exists and two strings suffice; *repainting the model's texture* -- the texture is
shared by every creature and the friend's; *drawing a player head model* -- the mask
is a flat face in a socket, not a head.

**Links**: `Aberrant.setFace/face`, `client/Skin.maskFront`, `client/RigDrawer.drawQuad`,
`client/AberrantRenderer`, the booth's `booth-face-stolen` frame.
