# D-0011 -- The creature's size is the profile's: every number tied to the body scales with it, in data or from the rules, never as a constant beside it

**Decision**: Rusty asked for the Face-Stealer one and a half times bigger (2026-09-15). The
size is the profile's `scale` (a sixteenth and a half a model unit) and the body's boxes; every
other number tied to the body follows from those rather than being a second constant that has
to be kept in step:

- The feet's measures -- how far an anchor drifts before the foot steps, how far under the
  rest point ground is looked for, how high a step lifts, how far ahead it lands -- move out of
  `Legs` into the profile's `rig.gait` (`stride`, `reach`, `lift`, `lead`, blocks).
- The strike's budget is a straight section's worth under the body's crawl rules
  (`Crawl.strikeBudget`): 32 at the model's own size, the number the first release had as a
  constant; 82 at one and a half.
- The way is planned for the body's rules (`Burrow.plan(..., Crawl.Rules, ...)`): it keeps
  the bore's reach from anything hard or wet (`Crawl.Rules.margin`, the cells the bore
  reaches out from the axis: one at a bore of one and a half, two at two and a quarter)
  instead of the fixed "one cell beside", and it rates an air cell cheap when a face lies
  within the body's hold (`Crawl.Rules.hold`, the whole cells of the clearance and one more:
  three) instead of only when a face touches it -- inside a five-by-four bore the middle rows
  touch nothing, so the first 1.5x build planned along the bore's ceiling, a row above
  where the head rides, and the head stood blocked wishing upward.
- A waypoint or a target under the head's feet, on the face it rides, counts as reached
  (`Crawl.reaches`): the axis rides its clearance over a player's feet or a sound in the
  floor, and at a clearance over the old reach of one and a half it would never have
  arrived and stood blocked, replanning.
- A wish into the face that cannot be taken is followed along whatever of it lies in the
  face rather than refused outright: a head riding 2.18 off a wall, half a block under its
  top, wishing at the way's next cell over the edge, read that wish as "into the wall" and
  stood blocked; now it climbs the last half block and wraps over.
- A leap lands when the head has come down to its riding height over a face (its clearance
  and this tick's approach), not when a face is within one tick's travel plus a hair -- which
  at the old clearance were the same number, and at the new one overshot the aim by 3.5.
- The maw is kept in model units and scaled by the profile at use; the pincers' reach, the
  spawn pocket (five across, seven into the wall), the cull reach and the shadow are the
  body's.
- The undulation and gait defaults, and the domain's `FACE_STEALER` constants, are the
  Face-Stealer's at its size, and say so; the tests that need a body a sixteenth a unit carry
  their own fixture.

**Why**: the plan's numbers were written at a sixteenth a unit and some of them lived in Java
beside the profile (the maw over sixteen, a grab reach of three, a strike budget of thirty-two,
a pocket of three, the legs' stride and reach). Multiplying each by one and a half would have
worked once and lied the next time the profile moved; deriving them from the profile's scale
and the crawl's rules makes the profile the one place a creature's size is decided, which is
what D-0001 promised.

**Rejected**: scaling nfx's model file (the file is kept as received, D-0002); a bare 1.5
multiplier on each constant; letting the strike cut whatever it readies with no budget (a
bend's tube is a hundred and nine cells at once); a Euclidean margin in the planner (exact for
the bore's corners but dearer per cell, and the Chebyshev margin is what "beside" already was).

**Consequences**: the speed (0.45 a tick) is unchanged and so reads slower against a body
half again as long; that is the tuning session's number, with the top speed, the pounce and
the dial. A plan pays a 125-cell scan per rock cell it passes instead of 27; the search's
table caches the kinds, so it is lookups, and the DEBUG line `planned a way of N cells in M us`
says what it costs in play.
