# D-0004 -- The creature keeps itself out of rock by the crawl, not by the game's physics; digs land on the strike's cue

**Decision**: the entity has no gravity, no block collision and is not pushable; the
pure `Crawl.step` moves its head's axis point each server tick, holding it its
clearance off one of six axis faces, turning within the face, climbing walls it meets,
wrapping over edges, boring into rock when it may dig, falling and attaching when it
has nothing. The entity's position is the axis less half its box; the face rides synced
data as its up. A way is `Burrow`'s A* over cells; the bore is `Tunnel`'s section; the
blocks are cut by `DigWorld` on the strike clip's cue, never straight from the crawl.
`Leap` gives a pounce's velocity, integrated as the game does, landing on the first
face flown into. Blocks are only ever read through `Cells`.

**Why**: vanilla `PathNavigation` plans an upright box over floors; it cannot walk a
wall or a ceiling, dig, or carry an eleven-block body, and it refuses a 2.5-wide mob
outright (phase 2 needed a scripted walk to move it at all). Axis faces make the crawl
exact and enumerable under JUnit (six cases; a slope is corners, as it is to a
centipede), where an arbitrary normal would need oriented boxes the engine cannot
collide. Cutting on the cue makes digging visibly the pincers' work and paces it (two
blocks a strike, six ticks): Rusty asked that it dig through with its pincers. Rock
beside anything hard or wet is never cut and never planned through, so a tunnel cannot
breach lava, water, bedrock or a chest.

**Alternatives rejected**: *vanilla navigation with a climbing flag* -- no ceilings, no
digging, no body; *arbitrary surface normals* -- unenumerable, and the game has no
oriented collision; *cutting blocks the tick the crawl asks* -- no animation beat, and
a dig faster than the eye.
