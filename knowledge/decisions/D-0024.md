# D-0024 — The band's top is -16, the chase 0.22

WAXER_01 kept a small mine and a bed in the shallow deepslate, and a Face-Stealer kept coming
up and killing him there. His save on the box (2026-11-01): bed at Y -9, standing at -15, last
death at -15, so his mine's floor is the row at -16. Rusty asked for the band to shrink from
-32..-8 to -32..-16 and for the creature to be "just a hair slower".

**The band.** `y_max` goes from -8 to -16 in the profile and in `Habitat.FACE_STEALER`;
`y_min` stays -32. Everything D-0020 and D-0023 hang on the band follows unchanged: spawning,
the body, digs, the hunt, the grab, the lurk. A creature saved inside the old top (-15 to -8)
is removed on its first tick after loading, without loot, as D-0020 removes any out-of-band
creature. Rusty was told -16 leaves WAXER's floor as the band's top row: standing at -15 he is
outside it and never taken up, but a step down puts him inside, and a creature that sees him
there lurks beside where he leaves (D-0023). He chose -16 over -18 and -20.

**The speed.** 0.24 to 0.22 blocks a tick (4.8 to 4.4 blocks a second), about 8% slower,
Rusty's pick of 0.21, 0.22 and 0.23. Still a little faster than a walking player (about 4.3),
well under a sprint (about 5.6). The quiet and wander multipliers and the pounce are unchanged.
Saved creatures take the new speed on load (D-0020).
