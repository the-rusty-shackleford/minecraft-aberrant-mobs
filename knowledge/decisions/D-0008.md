# D-0008 -- It spawns in the rock beside deep dark caves and persists once it knows you; sounds are CC0 recordings, nothing added

**Decision**: the entity type is offered to every overworld biome by a `neoforge:add_spawns`
modifier (weight 2, one at a time) and the spawn placement (`NO_RESTRICTIONS`, replaced)
accepts a site only for a profile whose habitat fits it: depth within its range, sky and
block light at most its bound (zero for the Face-Stealer), not the deep dark, a wall beside
the cave six thick ending in a pocket of rock, none of its kind within its exclusion, fewer
than its cap in the level. `finalizeSpawn` picks the fitting profile by weight, bores the
pocket quietly and moves the creature into it. It persists on entering stalk or hunt. Loot
is the profile's loot table plus the face it wore, one time in seven, with the victim's
profile on the item. Every sound is cut from a freesound.org CC0 recording by
`devtools/art/build.py`, credited in `SOURCES.md`, nothing synthesized, no filtering; the
cues play them and a skitter runs under way, quiet while stalking.

**Why**: Rusty: "roams only deep underground"; "Resident-Evil feel: scary, stalkery"; "a lit
base is safe: the fear stays outside the walls" (the plan). Spawning inside the wall means
the first sign of it is digging, which is the dread the brief asks for. Persisting once it
knows you keeps a stalk from evaporating with a chunk unload. Recordings over synthesis is
the lesson of the gun sounds (`gun-sounds-from-freesound-cc0`): synthesized creature
sounds read as arcade; cut field recordings do not.

**Alternatives rejected**: *spawning on the cave floor* -- seen at once, no dread;
*a fixed profile for natural spawns* -- the protocol has many creatures; the habitat on
the profile decides; *the deep dark* -- the warden's; *synthesized sounds* -- see the
guns.
