# Progressive 512-block hearing

Rusty requested a 512-block hearing limit, a very vague distant direction, a useful
general bearing around 256 blocks, and exact sound localisation within 64. They
explicitly endorsed sustained noise: each additional block break slightly improves
the estimate while the creature approaches and becomes more accurate with distance.

The game-event source model remains D-0005's: no silent-player position polling for
hearing, no response to other mobs or the creature's own digging, and one record per
source. A single block break has approximately 256 blocks of lateral uncertainty at
512 blocks and 47 at 256. The distance curve reaches zero at 64. Loudness moderates
the estimate without making a single distant explosion pinpoint its source.

Each further same-source noise contributes capped evidence. One block per second
reaches maximum confidence after approximately fifty seconds, halving uncertainty;
long range still remains imprecise. Duplicate events in the same tick add no evidence.
After four seconds of silence confidence fades over a minute. The last sound is
forgotten after two minutes. A source displaced more than 64 blocks from its previous
sound loses the accumulated estimate. Out-of-order sounds cannot replace newer ones.

The lateral error has a stable seeded side and remains horizontal. A random vertical
error can invent an unreachable destination outside the creature's narrow habitat;
the new model preserves the sound's height instead. Continued observations and the
listener's approach tighten the bearing, rather than rerolling it periodically.

This operates on existing loaded creatures. Natural spawn rules, caps, territory and
chunk simulation remain unchanged. The larger hearing radius alone does not ensure
an active creature is present or force distant chunks to tick.

Partitioned domain tests cover range boundaries, the three requested distance bands,
sustained/interrupted/relocated sources, same-tick events, competition and invalid
inputs. A real-server test repeatedly destroys blocks 500 blocks from a creature and
observes the game-event adapter's improving estimate, then confirms exact near hearing.
