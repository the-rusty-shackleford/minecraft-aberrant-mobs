# D-0020 — Enforce the underground habitat throughout a creature's lifetime

Rusty's friends encountered Face-Stealers at the surface. Rusty approved a deepslate
band above the lowest mining depths, excluding the Deep Dark, with the entire body,
digs, movement and attacks confined to it. They delegated the precise depths: -32
through -8. They explicitly required existing creatures to acquire the behavior,
or be removed, and subsequently requested a chase that a sprinting player can escape.
This supersedes D-0008's spawn-only habitat behavior; its spawn pocket, rarity,
loot and recording decisions continue, with the later size and audio decisions.

The habitat is read from the current profile for saved and new creatures alike.
An invalid loaded creature releases its passenger and is discarded before AI or
animation cues, without loot/XP. Valid saved creatures keep their current health;
updating speed attributes must not heal them. No persistent exemption, migration
flag or scan of unloaded chunks is used. Commands and eggs are also constrained.

Spawn pockets and body extents fit entirely inside the inclusive block rows. The
planner and final dig mutation reject out-of-band cells and the Deep Dark. Each
actual movement and pounce sample is checked with a body/ripple margin, and placed
multipart boxes are checked before animation cues. Terrain attachment uses the
unrestricted physical terrain rather than fictitious solid boundary planes. Biome
volume checks cover all quart samples the fuzzy biome lookup might select; this
conservative buffer avoids a segment overlapping the excluded biome.

Daytime sky exposure at any segment deals two health points every twenty ticks.
Sunlight has a dedicated damage type and goes directly through the ordinary living
damage pipeline, bypassing the carapace routing. It is not tagged as fire: the
entity type's existing fire immunity would otherwise prevent all sunlight damage.
Cover, night and rain protect it; torches do not injure an existing creature.

The shipped sustained speed is 0.24 blocks/tick instead of 0.45. Existing quiet and
wander multipliers remain, as do the charge and short-range pounce. A real-server
test drives vanilla sprint travel while the actual hunt AI pursues: the old speed
catches the player, the new speed allows the player to open a gap.

Before implementation, five shipped-profile server tests reproduced the missing
saved-state removal, both out-of-band pounces and sunlight damage. The complete
server suite subsequently passed all 51 cases, with 131 domain tests. These are
session-run checks, not inherited verification. Client and release evidence is
recorded in PROJECT.md as completed.

Surface protocol fixtures are generated from the shipped profile with only the
habitat removed. They remain test-only; dedicated habitat tests use the shipped
profile unchanged. This preserves motion/animation coverage without a production
switch that lets Face-Stealers evade their territory.
