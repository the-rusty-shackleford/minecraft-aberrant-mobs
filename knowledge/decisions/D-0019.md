# D-0019 — Smooth the visible pose and animate in the supporting surface's axes

Rusty's personal 1.3.0 playtest found jarring turns and broken walking animation away
from ordinary ground. They approved a roughly half-second camera transition with
responsive look, normal surface-relative animation, moving visual checks, and an
updated personal playtest. This supersedes D-0009's frame-only visual blend; the
six-axis physics and D-0018's deliberate controls remain unchanged.

The real-input shader baseline measured a 90-degree camera jump and 2.24-block eye
movement in one rendered frame. The old blend read the already-updated frame for
both endpoints. Eye position was never blended. During vertical wall movement, the
walking speed decayed to zero: vanilla's gait and body-turn calculations measured
world XZ, while the custom mover never advanced the walking-distance counter.
The existing settled-pose booth passed despite both failures.

`PoseBlend` is a pure immutable correction between complete visible poses. At a
surface change, the camera/body retain their last displayed world orientation and
position, then fade the rotation/translation correction over ten ticks. The current
physical target continues to receive mouse and movement input. A second change
starts at the current displayed pose. Local interpolation origins are rebased when
stance coordinates change, so a change of basis is not counted as walking or a
second turn. Body display history is weakly held and reset on world changes; a long
absence or teleport resets stale history. Camera collision clips the path from the
last safe displayed eye, not from a potentially incomplete new physical stance.

Tracked gravity can arrive before its matching stance position during ceiling armour
removal. Presentation holds its last valid pose until the new physical box fits.
This was caught by the added release-transition check: clipping from the interim
upright eye had moved the camera above the ceiling. A two-tick history timeout also
reproduced snapping under slower shader rendering; normal low-frame-rate gaps now
retain their transition history. The settled six-axis gate also caught a stationary
entity retaining its old-axis cached bounding box after a tracked gravity update
(`cachedFits=false`, `frameFits=true`). The presentation readiness check constructs
the current frame's box, preserving the incomplete-stance guard without holding an
already valid pose indefinitely.

The living-entity adapters supply surface-local displacement to vanilla's walking
speed easing and body-heading calculation. The mover advances walk/bob distance from
actual tangential travel, excluding stance relocation. The model and animation packs
retain their usual animation pipeline and receive the corrected inputs. No authored
models or resource-pack animation files are changed.

`PoseBlendTest` partitions old/new gravity, rebased look, endpoints, live input,
interrupted turns and invalid timing. `PresentationBooth` is a real-client gate for
camera continuity, connected and exposed corners, six-direction walking equivalent
to ground, actual rendered limb motion, Jump detach and armour removal. It uses the
production input/movement path and supports the player's installed animation stack.
Dedicated-server tests cannot substitute for that renderer/input gate.

The 1.3.0 candidate remains local and unreleased. Final verification is recorded in
PROJECT.md; this approval authorizes implementation and personal testing, not release.
