# D-0025 — The pincers reach through one block of rock, bursting it first

The Face-Stealer kept killing WAXER_01 in his mine, and grabbed him "through the ground". The
grab measured only distance: anything within 4.5 blocks of the head's axis (plus the prey's
width) was taken, whatever stood between. The stalk's rule `target.distance < 6 &&
!target.in_sight` grabs exactly when the prey cannot see it, so a stalker under a floor several
blocks thick took the player through it unseen and unheard.

Rusty: one block, bursting through, "but the player should be able to hear it coming at that
point and if they react quickly enough, can put enough distance to thwart the grab".

**The line.** `domain/Reach` walks the straight line from the head's axis to the prey's feet,
middle and eyes through the habitat's cells, the head's own cell left out, and takes the best:
CLEAR with nothing solid on a line; WALL with exactly one cell, ROCK and dry (`Tunnel.cuttable`);
BLOCKED otherwise (two or more, obsidian and the other HARD cells, wet rock, anything outside the
band, which habitat-aware cells read as HARD).

**The burst.** CLEAR, the pincers close at once, as before. WALL, `Aberrant.grab` breaks that
cell loudly (the block's own break, `dig_loud`, the screech), stops the crawl and refuses another
grab for `BURST_TICKS`, 20 ticks; at its end the pincers close only if the prey is still in reach
with nothing between. In a second a sprinting player covers about 5.6 blocks against a reach of
about 5.1 from the head, so one who reacts gets clear. BLOCKED, nothing happens.

**The tree.** A new sense, `target.reachable`: in reach and not BLOCKED, whether or not the
creature already holds someone. The three grab rules (stalk, hunt, lurk) wait on it, so prey
behind thick rock falls through to the approach or the chase, which digs closer. It must stay true
while one is held: the hunt's bite rule is a cooldown that falls through to the grab rule between
bites, and a false there sends the creature pouncing and chasing with its prey in its jaws,
interrupting the bite (a GameTest caught it while this was built).

The lurk keeps D-0023's post, a wall one thick from the shaft: the prey coming back is now burst
at, not taken through the wall.
