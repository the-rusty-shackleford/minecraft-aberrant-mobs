# D-0005 -- Hearing is the game-event stream with an error that scales with distance, never a poll of players

**Decision**: creatures hear through NeoForge's `VanillaGameEvent`: every game event a
player causes, or that nothing causes (a blast), within 320 blocks becomes a `Sound`
(position, loudness, tick, source) fed to each creature's pure `Hearing`. Loudness: a
step 1, a sprinting step 2, a block 6, a blast 20, a sneaking step nothing; other mobs
and the creature's own digging (the dig's game event names it as the cause) are ignored.
The estimate is the remembered sound loudest for its distance; its error is distance x
0.5 / loudness, capped at the distance, zero within 24 blocks; the bearing is the true
point moved sideways by the error along a direction drawn from the creature's seed and
re-drawn every 600 ticks; sounds are forgotten after 2400 ticks. The mind reads it as
`heard.any/bearing/error/age/distance/loud`; the prowl mode heads for the bearing and
the estimate sharpens as it closes.

**Why**: Rusty asked for "freakishly long range hearing, say several hundreds of blocks
... accuracy inversely proportional to distance ... a vague idea ... heads in that
direction ... eventually knows exactly where the player is". Polling players' positions
would make silence worthless; the game already says what makes noise, and sneaking is
free of it, so the mechanic teaches itself. A sideways offset keeps the bearing's sign
right (the creature always heads the right way, just not straight), and re-rolling the
direction slowly makes the guess drift rather than jitter, which reads as a creature
that is unsure, not one that is broken.

**Alternatives rejected**: *polling `level.players()` with noise* -- no silence; *a
random offset in any direction* -- the creature would sometimes head away; *per-tick
random* -- a jittering target the crawl could never settle on.
