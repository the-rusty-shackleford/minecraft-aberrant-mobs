# D-0016 — Chitin follows vanilla’s pixel shapes; new sounds remain recordings

**Decision:** generate chitin armour from the installed game’s netherite item and
armour-layer templates, preserving silhouettes, shade order and UV coverage while
recolouring to the creature’s violet-grey palette. Chitin and Cracked Carapace use
armadillo and turtle scute shapes; the crack and stepped bone-mask sprite are authored
in the generator. The creature’s supplied Blockbench project remains verbatim.
`devtools/art/SOURCES.md` identifies the underlying Mojang artwork separately from
our source licence. The deterministic generator reads templates from the prepared
game jar; no unmodified template is vendored.

**Why:** Rusty asked for the items and armour to belong to vanilla’s visual family.
The old flat blocks of colour lacked its layered silhouettes and shading. Comparing
front, side and back armour renders against netherite at identical camera positions
checks actual shoulders, waist, knees and boots, in addition to enlarged sprite pairs.

**Audio:** a CC0 chitter by taure becomes an alternate click; a CC0 low vibration
recording by lori.mortimer replaces the idle breath. Both are cut, faded and normalized
without synthesized tones, filtering or reverb. Source intervals and credits live in
`devtools/art/sounds/SOURCES.md`. D-0012’s rare, irregular breath timing remains.
Their perceptual suitability awaits Rusty’s listening review; decoding and runtime
resolution establish file validity, not scariness. The generator’s typing refactor was
verified to preserve all twelve old recordings byte-for-byte before these edits.
