# D-0012 -- The crawl follows the way's shape: it climbs only where the way rises, and lets go of a face it cannot follow the way from

**Decision**: the crawl's "rock ahead is a wall to climb" reflex fires only when the way it
follows rises off the face it clings to (`Aberrant.wayRises`, passed to `Crawl.step` as
`mayClimb`); for what the way goes round it turns where it stands until the line ahead is
clear. And a head on a wall or a ceiling whose wish leaves the face with no other face along
it in reach lets go and falls, landing on a floor within its clearance and a fall; a floor
holds it, since it cannot fly; a falling head never grabs a wall on the way down, so it is not
back on the one it let go of. A scripted walk, with no way, climbs whatever it meets, as
before.

**Why**: Rusty's first surface test at 1.5x (2026-09-15) had the creature stuck on trees and
flipped over. Reproduced in the booth on a lone oak and a one-block stone pillar, the two
symptoms were one mechanism: the way went round the pillar, the pillar sat on the head's
heading line, the head climbed it, went over and started down the far side; halfway down,
2.8 blocks up, the way's next cell was on the ground beyond, the wish turned away from the
pillar's face, no other face lay within the two-block attach reach, and the head was refused
and held there -- coiled round the post, head down, replanning every five ticks and breathing
every ninety. At the model's own size the same refusal happened 1.5 blocks up, within reach,
so it mostly got away with it.

**Rejected**: making leaves and logs air to the crawl and the planner (a `passable` tag): it
would also make a tree a refuge nothing can reach, and the ground-level way under a canopy
is walkable once the crawl stops climbing what the way goes round; a geometric "post versus
wall" test on the block ahead: the way already knows.

**And a waypoint inside the head's own clearance is passed** (`Aberrant.wish`, the reach
that was a fixed 1.2): with the lookahead grown to 2.4, the cell right before a post the way
goes round was never come within 1.2 of, and the head stood before the post upright and
refused, which the first rerun showed.

**Consequences**: a creature under a canopy wanting the ground drops to it; a stalker on a
cave wall wanting what is out on the floor beyond its attach reach drops to the floor rather
than standing refused; the two booth scenes (the oak, the pillar) stay as the regression
check. The breath cue, which made a stuck creature pant like a dog every four and a half
seconds, is now a low breath at odds of one in three hundred a tick while still.
