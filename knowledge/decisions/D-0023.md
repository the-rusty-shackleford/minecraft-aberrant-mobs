# D-0023 — Prey that leaves the band is lurked for where it left, not chased into it

Rusty, with a Face-Stealer rigid beside their mining shaft: "face stealer right near my
position, it seems to be stuck. Why?" It was hunting them at y −33 from y −21. D-0020 fences
the body inside y −32 through −8; the hunt's chase asks the dig planner for a way to the prey's
cell, the planner refuses every cell outside the band, and with no path the crawl moves by
zero. The creature stood at the boundary with a stale heading, replanning into nothing, until
its memory of the prey ran out. Asked for something scarier than pacing the floor, Rusty chose
the ambush over shadowing from above and a grudge that never forgets.

**Seeing them go.** D-0020 had the senses take up only players whose whole box lies inside the
band, so prey that stepped out simply vanished: the memory kept their last spot inside the
band, which read as in band, and the stalk walked to it. The senses still take up prey only
inside the band; the prey they have is no longer lost for stepping out of it, only for going
out of range. `target.in_band` says which, judged the way the grab and the pounce judge it, and
by the last known point when the ears are all that is left.

**The lurk.** A new mode. The moment hunt or stalk knows a prey outside the band, it sets a
five-minute `ambush` clock and enters `lurk`: a quiet, digging approach to `ambush.post` and
nothing else. In stalk that comes before the sight rules, so a prey watching from below is
lurked for, not climbed away from.
The post is just past the corridor's reach into the nearest wall thick enough for a pocket
beside `target.last_in_band`, the last spot the prey stood inside the band (the shaft or stair
they went down by), found by the spawn code's wall search (`Habitat.siteInWall`) at
`Habitat.ambushDepth(bore)`: one more than the crawl's bore rounded up, four cells for the
shipped body (its bore is 2.25), so the cell against the shaft is never cut and a wall one
block thick stays between the head and the prey, nothing to see from the shaft. The grab
reaches 4.5 from the head's axis, so prey in the shaft is within it. Never having had the prey inside the band, the anchor is their last known spot
brought inside it by the body's margin; with no wall thick enough, the anchor itself. The post
is cached per anchor: the search reads a few hundred cells and the anchor stands still for a
lurk. Habitat-aware cells read rock below the floor as hard, so a pocket that would straddle
the band's edge is refused there too.

At the post it holds. Prey back inside the band within six blocks is grabbed from the wall
(the stalk's own rule); back inside but farther is stalked again; staring at it starts the
hunt. Hurt hard, it flees. Once the prey is no longer known it leaves for roam when the
ambush clock runs out: five minutes from taking the post at most, at once if that has passed.
While it knows the prey it waits, however long. A lurker persists like a stalker; it makes no
entry sound, and at the post only the breath it makes when still.

**The way out of a chamber.** The scene's first real run stood the creature refused in its own
pocket for six hundred ticks, digging nothing: the planner's cheapest way to the post went
straight up the pocket's headroom (air by a face, the ceiling within its hold) and then west,
and a head on a floor has no way up open air, so the wish pointed along its own up and the
step was blocked, replan after replan. The planner now charges a vertical move between two
air cells at the air-with-none rate unless a wall stands beside the destination
(`Burrow.wallBeside`): rising or dropping through air is done along a wall. A shaft, walled
all round, is still climbed at the air rate; the way out of a chamber leaves along its floor
to a wall, or digs. That is a fault older than the lurk (any prey above a cave floor across
open air), found because the lurk's post is inside rock at the prey's height.

Rejected: shadowing the prey along the band's floor (audible, but it tells them exactly where
it is); a grudge that never forgets (a second feature, later if wanted); sealing the corridor
behind itself (the pocket already opens on no cave, and the dig cannot know which ore it cut);
a config for the wait (the tree's number is the tuning knob, as for patience).

**Verification.** `LurkTest` ticks the shipped tree through every partition (entered from hunt
and from stalk, grabbed, stalked again, hunted on a stare, held while known for seven thousand
ticks, left after six thousand unknown, fled when hurt hard, the vocabulary); `HabitatTest`
places the post three into the wall, across a cave, refuses a thin wall and a pocket straddling
the floor. The habitat gametest `preyThatLeavesTheBandIsAmbushedWhereItLeft` runs the scene on
a real server with the shipped profile: seen in the shaft, gone below, lurked, the post beside
the shaft, the head there, the wall intact, the prey back up and taken.
